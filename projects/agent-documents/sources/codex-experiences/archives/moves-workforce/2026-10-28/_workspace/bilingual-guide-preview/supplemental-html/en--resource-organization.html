Purpose and access

Organization overview shows people through the team hierarchy. Resource management read is required. This is a read-only view. Creating teams, changing leaders or parents, and allocating members are performed in Settings → Team management with Team management write.

Visible teams and people follow the applicable organization or team-lead scope, including permitted child teams.

[Image insertion point · additional capture needed] Team tree, direct-versus-total headcounts, direct-members toggle, and the selected team's people list.

Team tree and member list

The left tree shows the organization, teams, and child teams. It identifies the leader and direct and cumulative headcounts. Use the direct-members toggle to include or exclude child-team members.

The right list shows names, team, job role, level, aggregated utilization, and skills. The leader appears first with a leader badge and is counted as a member of the team.

Missing or incomplete results

If some people have not been aggregated, the screen reports their exclusion. Read that notice before comparing totals. An empty team is not automatically an error; allocate members in Team management if needed. Edit the hierarchy in settings rather than this screen.

Organization overview versus team analysis

Organization overview answers who belongs to a team through individual rows. Team analysis compares teams, resource indicators, and hierarchical rollups.

Buttons, fields, and result checks

Organization overview displays team hierarchy and permitted members, separately from editing organizational settings.
