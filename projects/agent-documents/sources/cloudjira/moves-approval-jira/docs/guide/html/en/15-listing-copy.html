Moves Approval for Jira — Listing copy

Tagline

Review worklogs, track approval decisions, and manage period closing in Jira Cloud.

Summary

Approve individual Jira worklogs with a shared inbox, decision history and period controls. Use standalone teams or optionally connect Moves Workforce.

More details

Bring worklog review into Jira Cloud. Authors save work and track approval requests, while assigned approvers review individual records in a shared inbox. Keep approval decisions and observed worklog changes visible, configure automatic requests and period closing, and manage eligible record exports. Approved worklogs are locked against edits through the app. Administrators can configure additional worklog controls and must verify their scope before rollout. Use Approval on its own or pair it with Moves Workforce for linked issues and team information. English and Korean translations are included. Current onboarding focuses on individual worklog approval; period timesheet submission and status change approval are not presented as available release features.
