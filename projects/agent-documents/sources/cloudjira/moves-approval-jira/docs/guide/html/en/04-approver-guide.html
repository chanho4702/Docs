Approver guide

  1. Open Apps → Moves Approval → My turn and select a request.
  2. Check its type, author, issue, work start time, duration, reason and approval line.
  3. Choose Approve or Reject. Supply a rejection reason and signature PIN when policy requires them.
  4. Check the outcome. For a status change request, read approval status and Jira application status separately.

An action can be rejected if permissions, deadlines or another approver's decision changed while the page was open. Refresh and check the current state. Being a CC recipient alone does not grant decision rights.

Bulk decisions

Select eligible requests to approve them together. Each request is validated separately at execution time. If eight out of ten succeed, review the two failures rather than repeating the successful decisions.

History, signatures and delegation

Review completed decisions in your history and use the Excel export or approval certificate where available. Manage downloaded files under your organization's retention policy.

Use signatures, PINs and delegation only when enabled by the administrator. Delegated decisions record the actual actor and still require Jira visibility. Do not share your PIN or signature image.
