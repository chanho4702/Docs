# Approver and Cc recipient guide

## Approve or reject

1. Open **Moves Approval → My turn** from Jira's app navigation and open a request.
2. Review the issue, submission, change notices and approval line. For timesheets, distinguish current totals from the submitted snapshot.
3. Choose **Approve** or **Reject**. Rejection reasons are required by default.
4. Enter your signature PIN if the policy requires it.
5. Check the result and next step. Refresh if another approver already decided or the deadline has passed.

**Handled** shows requests you decided on, **In progress** shows requests waiting on an earlier step, and **Cc** shows requests you were copied on. Cc status does not enable decision buttons. Lists and details respect your current Jira issue visibility.

## Bulk approval

Select requests currently waiting on you and approve them together. Check the success and failure counts; one failure does not mean every request failed. Team leads can also [approve selected timesheets](cloudjira--05-timesheet-guide.md). For field changes, verify application status separately after bulk approval.

## Signature, PIN and delegation

Register your signature image and PIN under **My signature**. PINs contain 4–12 digits. Five failed attempts lock the PIN for 15 minutes. Do not share it.

If delegation is enabled, use **My delegation** to assign a delegate and validity period. Eligible delegated decisions record the actual person who acted. Delegation does not grant issue visibility.

## Notifications and certificates

Use in-app notifications for requests, decisions and reminders. Transition approvals may also create Jira @mention comments when policy and comment permissions permit. Jira email and push delivery depend on Jira notification settings.

Download an available certificate PDF from a completed request or export the inbox to Excel. Share only the required scope; these downloads are customer-managed copies.
