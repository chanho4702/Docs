Choose a period to review your work and effort, then check your current permissions and skills. Registered team leaders can also review their teams in **My Team**.

## Review your contribution

1. Open My Page and choose this month, this week, or a custom period.
2. Select a participating project if you want to narrow the tasks, charts, and worklogs.
3. Review work status and time recorded in the period, then inspect individual issues when needed.
4. In your permissions section, check the applied permission set and project visibility scope.
5. Keep your skills up to date in your skills section.

Available skills come from the administrator-managed dictionary. Ask an administrator to add a missing skill. Skills are used for resource search and project assignment matching.

## For team leaders

**My Team** depends on your actual team-leader assignment, not the name of a permission set. Use it to review work, worklogs, and utilization for the teams you lead and their subteams.

- You can edit team-member skills within the scope allowed to you as a leader.
- Changing roles, levels, salaries, or permissions requires the relevant administrative access.
- When several members work on the same issue, a team's issue count may differ from the sum of individual counts.

## Choose the right team view

| Question | View |
|---|---|
| What is the team I lead working on? | My Team on My Page |
| Which teams actually contributed to this project? | Project team analysis |
| How do workloads compare across teams and projects? | Team analysis in Resource Management |

If My Team is missing, check the leader assignment in [team management](cloudjira--admin-people-teams.md). If figures differ between readers, compare periods, project filters, and visibility first.

## Buttons, fields, and result checks

My Page shows your projects, contribution, permissions, skills, and permitted team information. The date filter does not apply identically to every card.

| Control or field | Function, conditions, and data affected | Result check and recovery |
|---|---|---|
| Start / end dates | Limit your worklogs/time trends to a period. My Issues follows assignee/collaborator relationships in selected projects and is not restricted by the date filter. | Do not compare issue count and period worklogs as if they used the same population. |
| Project search, active only, select all, clear | Find projects and choose the ones to analyse. Clearing selection does not delete data. | With no project selected, issue/worklog panels can show a selection prompt. |
| Issue status, role, due date, search | Narrow the list by status, assignee/collaborator role, overdue/due-in-seven-days/no-due-date, and key/title search. | No filtered results does not mean the Jira issues were deleted. |
| Worklog list / by issue / search | View your own entries as a flat list or grouped by issue. Other authors' time on the same issue is not included. | Review grouped totals with details and check date, issue, and description search conditions. |
| Excel | Export your worklogs for selected projects/period. This does not modify entries or create Jira worklogs. | Check filters and the Summary, Worklogs, and By Issue sheets. Use Worklog Export for broader extraction. |
| Completion rate, daily average, contribution charts | Completion rate is completed issues divided by assigned issues. Daily average divides logged time by dates with entries; dates without logs are excluded. | Distinguish these from effort-weighted progress and averages over all working days. Long-range daily charts can show only dates with entries. |
| Edit/add/save/cancel skills | Select your skills from the dictionary and save. Category filters narrow choices without creating/deleting dictionary entries. | Check the selected list after saving. Cancel discards unsaved selection changes; request missing dictionary skills from an authorized administrator. |
| Permission search / read-write filter | Inspect the permissions applied to you. Searching/filtering this list does not change permissions. | Request changes from an administrator. Check both menu access and actual data scope. |
| My team / include sub-teams / member skills | Team leaders may see permitted team information and member-skill editing. Leadership alone does not allow unrestricted salary, permission, or level changes. | Check descendant inclusion and aggregation time. Values can differ from organization team analytics because live data and completion formulas differ. |
