Let another person handle your approval turns while you are away. Administrators must enable delegation first.

Location: Approval inbox → My delegation.

Register a delegation

  1. Review any existing delegation under the section for requests you delegate.
  2. Choose a Delegate, enter the start and end dates, and add an optional note.
  3. Save, then recheck the person and period.
  4. Ask the delegate to confirm they can open the target pages. Delegation does not grant Confluence page permission.
  5. Use Remove delegation to end it early.

Screenshot · MA-09 — Delegate, dates, note, and the list of people who have delegated to the current user.

Decide as a delegate

Check who has delegated to you. During the valid period, their turns appear in My turn with a delegate indicator. Review the page and version, then approve or reject normally.

Item Meaning
Delegation period Must be active at the time of the decision.
Name on the approval line Remains the original approver.
Actual decision maker Recorded as the delegate as well.
Policy disabled Saved delegations have no effect.

Troubleshooting

For missing requests, check dates → delegation policy → the original approver's active turn → page view permission → inbox filters. Delegation does not skip earlier steps or extend deadlines.
