## What to include

- Your Confluence site (`name.atlassian.net`) and app version
- What you were doing, which screen or message appeared, and the steps to reproduce
- Screenshots if possible. **Please hide approval content and personal information.**

We do not access app data on customer sites, so please send us what we need to investigate. Never send passwords or API tokens.

## Reporting a security vulnerability

Report security issues to [security@dmove.kr, or support@dmove.kr with "[Security]" in the subject]. We acknowledge reports within [2] business days and keep you updated until the issue is resolved. Please do not disclose the issue publicly before we confirm a fix.
