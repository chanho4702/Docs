Moves Workforce is a Jira app for assigning people and work to projects and reviewing effort, utilization, progress, and labor cost using recorded work. New users can start with the quick start. Administrators should begin with initial setup.

Get started

  • Quick start: open the app, record your first worklog, and check your project.
  • Administrator setup: prepare the app for your users.
  • Glossary: distinguish projects and spaces, MD and MM, and progress measures.

Get help

Use the symptom-based checks in the FAQ. If a menu is missing, check app permissions. If an issue is missing or an update is rejected, also check your Jira permissions for that issue.

Open Moves Workforce from the Jira Apps menu. The Confluence space containing this guide hosts documentation; the app runs in Jira.
